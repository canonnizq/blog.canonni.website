toki a! I'm [CanonNi](https://canonni.website), and [The Canonnical Blog](https://blog.canonni.website) is, shockingly, my blog. I started it in late August 2024, and, like many other things in my life, abandoned it a week later. After a year of collecting digital dust it was brought back to life in September 2025, before another (hopefully longer-lasting) revival in 2026. So uh, bienvenidos! Welcome to my eclectic little corner on the vast ocean that is the internet.

## Abstract

All decent authors have a distinct style of writing. Hemingway preferred direct journalist-style lines, Joyce used flowery avant-garde language, and Christie was heavy on symbolism. I, however, am a horrible writer, and topics I cover in this blog are all over the place. To quote a good friend, "You write like an orangutan who just learned human language, and I absolutely love it."

**No generative AI is used to assist writing whatsoever. Everything you read is entirely human/orangutan.**

What can you expect? Well, not that I'm a white astrology girlie, but if there's one thing being a Sagittarius born on the same day as Mark Twain has brought me, it's the ability to hold a grudge for literal years. I have a homeopathic tendency to nitpick everything I encounter, and since I can't physically assault an inanimate object, most of the angst translates into my writings. Unlike Twain, however, I have never really succeeded in truly completing anything: my Notion project houses dozens of pieces of unfinished ledes and this blog, despite supposedly being a repertoire of my own thoughts, sees little more than a few posts a month.

The reason I named this The Canonnical Blog was initially just because it was a decent pun that sounded vestigial, but now that I think of it, it reflects its nature pretty well, as a weirdly personal and abstract platform for eclectic pieces of mini literature. Yay?

## Technologia!

Saluton, fellow 🤓. Want to start your own blog or try to hack mine? Here's the `README`, which may help when checking out the [source code](https://github.com/canonnizq/blog.canonni.website), though there's really not much to see beyond yaml and markdown files.

This site is built using [Hexo](https://hexo.io) and its community-made [Icarus](https://ppoffice.github.io/hexo-theme-icarus/) theme. I've made some manual tweaks here and there but most components are used straight out-of-the-box. It is deployed with Vercel, which also hosts my [Waline](https://waline.js.org/) instance powering this blog's comment section and some other gadgets I've made over time.
