So uh... In case you haven't noticed the fact that 2025's post count is 1, yeah, [that daily blogging promise](https://blog.canonni.website/2025/09/26/Back-online/) didn't exactly pan out. I brashly wrote that after listening to a lecture that, in retrospect, is textbook grade cult recruitment tactic, and never really intended to actually write to the two other people that actually visit this site.

*...but why am I writing this then? Ahhh, a paradox! Nisauce, Michael here...*

## Lead Vocals

Well, a rather heated altercation I was recently involved with sent me down a rabbit hole that led to one of the participants' personal blogs. It was comically poorly bodged together, posted barely copyedited articles only occasionally, and wasn't even HTTPS, but I ended up spending an entire evening reading every post of theirs in what can only be described as an idiosyncratic stupor. I have been instructed by a former debate teammate to refrain from swearing, so uh, let’s just say that I viscerally digress from their viewpoints and would rather slit my own throat than live in the society they envision. I have never felt more disgusted by text alone and if ragebating applied to literature, their content would be the *Arithmetica* of said skill. Any remotely self-dignifying platform or publication would’ve taken down the content at first sight, yet, as it was hosted on a private blog free of regulation, it remained in place as a covert landmine ready to detonate any unsuspecting visitor’s feeble mind. 

The most common descriptors friends and foes cast onto my *Pongo sapiens* soul are “eclectic” (fair), “what the actual f*ck man” (based), and “control freak” (ehh…). Back when I served as editor-in-chief and before they shooed me off for… the incident, I would meticulously scrutinize every sentence of every submission, relentlessly making remedies here and there until it satisfied my peculiar niche. This reached an extent where authors nicknamed me “CommaNi.” Yet, for all the marks my gritty little paws spread all over the parchment, I never made a single rejection whatsoever, even for two eloquent little pieces that attacked me by name. Borrowing the overused quote, “I disapprove of what you say, but I will defend to the death your right to say it.” Turning back to their abhorrent blog, I wish nobody ever reads what they write, but if their domain expires someday, I’ll be the first to pitch in for renewal.

Speaking of, in [my domain post](https://blog.canonni.website/2024/09/01/Owning-a-domain/) two years ago, I awkwardly phrased a certain concept as, "This means that, in theory, the entire internet is private property, and that, when I inevitably die[??], this domain and all of its contents are directly transferred to my next-in-line." I guess what past me wanted to feel poetic saying was something along the lines of, “one’s website is the digital equivalent to a private residence, in the sense that anything put inside is fully personal and subject to no oversight.” I mean… yeah, no shit sherlock. That’s quite literally the only reason people buy domains and build their own sites.

But what did I really mean? Over the years, I have tried a number of platforms and faucets to dump my work onto: Median, Notion, Reddit, Forums, just to name a few. They allow me to write and publish at virtually no cost, empowering me with a myriad of features a Hexo blog certainly lacks, but, as the nature of public platforms dictate, all content must conform to a conceptually narrow-minded algorithm or be buried in a mountain of wannabe doppelgangers. Even if my intended audience is only like-minded individuals I know personally, they’ll only be aware when I actively send them a link, and, I know this may sound a little too radical, I would preferably prefer not to beg for others to read. I’m not one of those degenerates who pay for engagement.

Phew. I guess that pretty much seals it then. This was my shower thought for a few days and I had come up with a few better organized points than an attack ad, but now that I think of it, that’s pretty much all I need. After all, wasn’t the first blog written an explicit call against the notion of blogs itself? The Canonnical Blog FTW…?

## Special: RecentChanges

Oh yeah, did I forget to mention this? The entire site has received a complete makeover from header to footer, now being deployed on the more feature-rich Vercel with a better optimized backend. The original theme, Fluid, felt too… watered-down [insert laugh track here], and the frontend has been migrated to [Icarus](https://ppoffice.github.io/hexo-theme-icarus/) thanks to its Material-esque feel as a result. I promise I’m not a corporate tool paid by Google. I’ve also drawn a pixelated logo to replace the copyrighted orangutan photo, though visual art is evidently not one of my talents.

As part of this migration, the original backend used for comments, [Valine](https://valine.js.org/), was found to have a security concern that allowed anyone with technical knowhow to dox any user and has since ceased operations. This site has never stored IP addresses and whoever “chad” is is safe, though, just to be sure, I have made a switch to the much more secure [Waline](https://waline.js.org) (I swear I'm not making these names up) hosted on a [private instance](https://waline.canonni.website). Comment away!

PS: To whoever the misspelt “Anonymus” was, life is what happened. 🤓


## Final thoughts

In an ephiphany of sorts tangentially related to the aforementioned previous post, I hooked up all of my sites to Google Analytics, which, amongst other gadgets, gives me the ability to visualize visits by location and time. Most visits are by scraping bots based in bumfuck nowhere in Russia and the Midwest, but for the small portion of organic humans(?) that pop by every now and then, Shanghai, HK, Tokyo, LA, NYC, and Singapore are hotspots thanks primarily to the fact that most commercial VPNs mask IPs as from there, as most of my former acquaintances remain within the glorious firewall. 

I don't really check this... ever, but as part of finalizing this site's migration, I had to scan the dashboard for the obscure button to get a new Tracking ID, and, in the corner of my eye, I caught a single pale blue dot from Southborough MA who clicked into every post and left without a word.

*...yeah.*
